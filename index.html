How it works

One dashboard, four moving parts.

Beacon doesn't replace your analytics — it pulls the pieces that live in five different tabs into one place you actually check.

01

Connect

Point Beacon at your site, Search Console, and analytics account once. Every teammate shares the same connection.

beacon.connect(site, integrations)
02

Track

Beacon checks your keyword positions daily and flags on-page issues — titles, headers, alt text — as they appear.

beacon.track_rankings(keywords)
03

Plan

Lay content and campaigns out on a shared calendar, with budget and channel attached to each entry.

beacon.plan_campaign(calendar, budget)
04

Report

See what's actually moving rankings and spend in one dashboard — no exporting into slides before the Monday meeting.

beacon.generate_report(range)
Features

Everything a search & campaign lead checks weekly.

Six pieces that usually live in six different tools, tracking the same site.

Keyword rank tracking

Daily position checks across the keywords that matter, split by device and location, with history you can actually trend.

On-page SEO audits

A running checklist against the things that actually move rankings — titles, headers, meta, alt text, internal links — scored per page.

Content calendar

Plan posts and pages by channel and owner, see what's due, and keep publishing cadence visible to the whole team.

Backlink monitoring

New and lost backlinks tracked automatically, with authority context so you know which ones are worth a thank-you.

Campaign & budget tracking

Spend, channel, and campaign goals in one place — cost per result sits next to organic performance, not in a separate sheet.

Social post scheduler

Queue and schedule posts across channels from the same calendar that drives the rest of your content plan.

Rank tracking

See exactly where you rank — and where you're headed.

Checked daily, not whenever you remember

Positions refresh every day per keyword, per device and location, so a ranking dip shows up before a client asks about it.

Tied to the page it's ranking for

Every keyword links back to the on-page audit for that URL, so a rank drop and a broken title tag show up in the same view.

Search Console does the crawling

Once connected, Beacon reconciles its own SERP checks against your real Search Console impressions and clicks — not a third-party estimate.
